1. Scope

This policy describes what data Beyond Copper Branding ("the Service") collects, how it is used, and your choices. It covers the Coordinator Portal and the Client Portal. The Service is intended for business use by the agency and its clients.

2. Data we collect

Account and access data. The coordinator's login credentials (stored securely; passwords are never displayed). Client portal access codes and the client records the coordinator creates (name or label, contact details the coordinator enters).

Uploaded content. Videos, images, thumbnails, captions, and other files clients or the coordinator submit for publishing.

Network connection data. When a client connects a YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, or Facebook account through that network's own login screen (OAuth), the Service receives and stores server-side access tokens and basic account identifiers needed to publish on the client's behalf. The Service never sees, asks for, or stores client passwords for any network.

Engagement data. Comments on published posts (used for first-hour replies), reply drafts and history, and per-post performance information pulled back from the networks to improve future publishing.

AI personalization data. Each client's locked niche, voice notes and sample captions, banned topics, and performance history — used to personalize AI-assisted replies, captions, and clip selection per client and per post.

Technical data. Basic logs needed to operate and secure the Service (such as access times and error logs).

3. YouTube API Services disclosure

The Service uses YouTube API Services to publish videos to YouTube on the client's behalf. By using the Service's YouTube publishing features, you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms), and Google's handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

YouTube API data the Service accesses, collects, and stores is limited to what publishing requires: the client's channel identity, upload status, and video metadata for videos published through the Service. This data is used only to perform and confirm publishing, and is shared only with YouTube via its API at the client's direction and with the Service's hosting infrastructure provider as needed to operate. You can revoke the Service's access to your YouTube data at any time via the Google security settings page at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions — revoking ends the Service's access. The Service never requests, collects, or stores your YouTube login credentials.

4. Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok data

Data received through Meta's and TikTok's platforms (account identifiers, access tokens, post and comment data needed for publishing and replies) is used only to provide the publishing service the client authorized — publishing videos, posting replies, and reporting performance. It is not sold, not shared with advertisers, not used to build profiles, and not used for any purpose other than operating the Service as described in this policy. Platform data is deleted when it is no longer needed for the service, when the client revokes access or deletes their account, or when the network requests deletion.

5. How we use data

  • Provide and operate the publishing service (intake, scheduling, publishing, reporting).
  • Generate AI-assisted comment replies, captions, clip selections, and niche proposals, personalized per client and per post.
  • Provide support, maintain security, and fix errors.
  • Communicate about the Service and the client's account.

6. AI and your data

Inputs processed by AI features (comments, captions, niche and voice information) are processed only to provide the requested feature. Client content is not sold and is not used to train generalized or public AI models. Any AI sub-processor is instructed to use data solely to provide the service and not to retain it for other purposes.

7. Sharing

Data is shared only:

  • With the social networks, via their official APIs, at the client's direction to publish content and replies.
  • With infrastructure providers that host and operate the Service (bound to use data only for that purpose).
  • When required by law, or to protect the security of the Service.
  • Personal data is never sold.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Uploaded files: automatically deleted 30 days after the video is published; portfolio packages 90 days after upload. These are working files, not an archive.
  • Access tokens: deleted when the client revokes access on the network side, disconnects the account in the Service, or closes their client record.
  • Account and engagement records: kept while the client relationship is active, then deleted on request or when no longer needed.

How to request deletion. Any user of the Service may request deletion of their data by contacting mjcopper80@outlook.com. Requests are honored promptly. Network-side revocation (for example, removing the app's access in Google or Meta settings) independently ends the Service's access to that network's data.

9. Security

The Service uses reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect data, consistent with industry standards. No system is 100% secure. The coordinator is responsible for keeping their password confidential and for using the Service's access controls; suspected compromise must be reported promptly to mjcopper80@outlook.com.

10. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact mjcopper80@outlook.com. The coordinator (agency) is responsible for handling data requests from its own clients and for having the necessary consents to provide client data to the Service.

11. Children

The Service is a business tool and is not directed to children. Users must be of legal age to enter a contract in their jurisdiction.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the Service or by email, and the effective date above will be revised. Continued use after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.

13. Contact

Privacy questions, complaints, or deletion requests: mjcopper80@outlook.com
