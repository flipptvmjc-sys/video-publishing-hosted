SLA queue — 3-day clock

Clock starts at intake and counts only unpaused time. Blocking pauses it; unblocking resumes it and moves the deadline by exactly the paused time. Sorted by time left.

Replies — draft first

AI-drafted replies land as drafts. Nothing posts without coordinator approval. “Mark posted” means you posted the approved reply yourself — no platform API is wired yet, so the record says manual.

AI jobs

Batch jobs through the Sophia wrapper (one at a time, 300 MiB gate). A gated job is an honest outcome — it was refused for memory, never forced.

Plans — versioned

A new version arrives as a draft with a visible diff. An approved version stays approved until you explicitly approve the new one — a new version never silently overwrites an approved caption or plan.

Trend feed — weekly network watch

Runs a network_watch batch job through the wrapper. The wrapper passes WebSearch/WebFetch for this job type, so items are researched on the web and graded “sourced” with the fetched URL; anything the model could not verify is graded “unverified” — that is the honest result, not a failure.

Publish queue — per platform

One record per platform, with its own state (draft / needs_approval / scheduled / sending / sent / error / failed) and its own retry (3 attempts, 10 minutes apart). A platform is only ever marked sent when the platform itself confirms the post. 3 of 4 live is the dangerous state — a video with some platforms sent and others failed is flagged here, never hidden. No platform adapter is enabled yet, so the worker refuses honestly until Michael gives the per-platform go-ahead (TikTok first).

Token health — three layers

Hourly sweep + pre-slot check + pre-call validation. A token near expiry is refreshed; a dead token marks the account EXPIRED, fails its due posts with the platform's own message, and shows a Reconnect chip here. Token values are never shown — only whether they exist and when they expire.

Comment intake — webhook

Comments and DMs arrive by Meta webhook (signature-verified, deduped by message id). Layer 1 keyword matches (price / link / info / available) get a one-sentence reply routing to DM, drafted instantly — still a draft until you approve it. Everything else enqueues an AI draft (see Replies). Nothing auto-posts. First-hour replies are timed from the video's published time.
