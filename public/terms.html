1. What this service is

Beyond Copper Branding ("the Service") is an agency-managed video publishing platform. It has two doors:

  • The Coordinator Portal (private) — used by the agency/coordinator to manage clients, intake files, schedule publishing, and review AI-assisted work.
  • The Client Portal — used by the agency's clients to submit videos, files, and information through a code-based link. Clients do not log in; they open the portal and type a code.

The Service coordinates the publishing of client-approved videos to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook. Publishing to each network is enabled per network as the connection is built, reviewed, and verified — the Service does not claim direct publishing to a network until that network's connection is live and verified.

2. The professional default

The Service operates hands-off by default: once a client provides final approval, files, and required information, the coordinator proceeds with publishing under professional judgment. Per-post approval is available as an opt-in setting, not the default. Silence from a client means proceed under the professional default unless the client has opted into per-post approval.
